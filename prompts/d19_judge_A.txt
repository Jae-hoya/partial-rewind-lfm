You judge whether an ANSWER addresses what the QUESTION asks. Do not judge whether the answer is true.

Labels:
- full: the answer makes a statement about exactly what the question asks for (a number, a period, a cause, a criticism, an effect ...). If the question asks for several things, directly answering one of them is enough.
- partial: the answer is related to the question but sidesteps its core (surrounding facts, background, general statements about the topic).
- none: the answer talks about the same topic but says nothing about what the question asks.
